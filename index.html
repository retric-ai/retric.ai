How engagements work

  1. Assess

    A short, concrete review of your delivery pipeline, cloud estate and on-call reality. You get a prioritized findings report — not a slide deck of buzzwords.

  2. Build

    Hands-on implementation alongside your team, in your repositories, through your review process. Everything as code, everything tested.

  3. Hand off

    Documentation, runbooks and pairing sessions so your engineers own the result. Success is when you no longer need us.
